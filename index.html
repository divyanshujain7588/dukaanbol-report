01

Aaj dukaandar ki problem

  • Bill haath se ya calculator se banta hai. Rush mein galti ho jaati hai.
  • Udhaar ek copy mein likha jaata hai. Copy kho gayi to hisab bhi gaya.
  • Kaunsa maal khatam ho raha hai, ye yaad se chalta hai.
  • Din ke end mein thak ke hisab milana mushkil lagta hai.
  • Asli profit kitna hua, ye pata hi nahi chalta.
  • Jo apps hain, unme bahut typing aur tapping hai. Customer saamne khada ho to time nahi hota.

06

Pricing: do strategies

Target customer dono mein same hai: chhote business aur dukaandar.

Strategy A

Free app, ads ke saath

₹0 / ads hatane ke liye ₹1,000 ya ₹2,000 per month

Fayde

  • Free hai, to log jaldi try karenge.
  • Zyada users jaldi aa sakte hain.
  • Ads se bhi thodi kamai.

Risk

  • Billing ke time ads aaye to dukaandar pareshan hoga.
  • Sirf ads hatane ke liye ₹1,000-2,000 dena mehnga lag sakta hai.
  • Paying users kam ho sakte hain.
Strategy B

₹99 ka 3-day trial

₹999 / month, trial ke baad

Fayde

  • Jo ₹99 deta hai, wo serious customer hai.
  • Pehle din se kamai.
  • Koi ads nahi, app saaf rehta hai.

Risk

  • ₹99 bhi pehli baar dene mein log hichkichate hain.
  • 3 din mein aadat banana mushkil.
  • Shuru mein users kam aayenge.
